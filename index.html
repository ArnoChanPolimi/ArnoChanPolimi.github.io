CV: I am a master's student in Telecommunication Engineering at Politecnico di Milano. I am carrying out my thesis in the BONSAI Lab, within the Department of Electronics, Information and Bioengineering (DEIB), under the supervision of Qiaolun Zhang and Massimo Tornatore.

My thesis focuses on distributed AI training across datacenters. I study how collective communication schedules and network routing can adapt together when available bandwidth changes. I work with PyTorch FSDP, NCCL/MSCCL, and RoCEv2 RDMA to build a testbed and measure how these decisions affect communication and training performance. I am interested in research and engineering opportunities in AI infrastructure and high-performance networking.

In autumn 2025, I spent a semester at ENSEA in France on an Erasmus exchange, studying computer networks, wireless communications, and network and software security.

Before my master's studies, I worked on carrier-grade gateway software, developing and testing C/IPsec functionality. I received my B.Sc. in Electronic Information Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology's School of Information and Electronics in 2023, where my undergraduate thesis with Qin Zhang covered cooperative transmission in wireless ad hoc networks.

Outside my studies, I enjoy traveling and keep a map of the places I have visited.

Research

Master's Thesis: Collective Communication and WAN Adaptation
I use PyTorch FSDP, NCCL/MSCCL, and RoCEv2 RDMA to study communication across datacenters. My thesis investigates how All-Gather schedules and network paths can be adjusted together as available bandwidth changes, using network measurements to guide and evaluate those decisions.
