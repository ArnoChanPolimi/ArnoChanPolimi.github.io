About me: I am a master's student in Telecommunication Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, working at the intersection of distributed AI systems and communication networks. My background spans networking software, wireless communication, signal processing, and machine learning, with a current focus on the infrastructure that supports distributed AI training.

My master's research explores collective communication across geographically distributed GPU workers. When training spans multiple datacenters, the wide-area network becomes part of the training system: changes in available bandwidth and path conditions affect how workers exchange data and synchronize. I study how collective schedules and network routing can adapt together to these changing conditions.

I build and evaluate end-to-end systems using PyTorch FSDP, NCCL/MSCCL, and RoCEv2 RDMA. My work connects All-Gather schedule design, per-channel multipath traffic steering, and network telemetry. This involves working across the training framework, communication libraries, and network configuration, then using measurements to understand how their interactions affect communication performance.

Before graduate research, I worked on carrier-grade gateway forwarding software, developing and validating C/IPsec functionality in a large production codebase. That experience gave me practical exposure to protocol implementation, debugging, and software validation, complementing my academic work on communication systems.

Alongside my networking research, I have worked on recommender systems, computer vision, contactless vital-sign sensing, and statistical learning. These projects range from hybrid recommendation models and video-based heart-rate estimation to FMCW radar signal processing and LoS MIMO capacity optimization. They give me experience with both the algorithms that process data and the systems that support their execution.

I am interested in internship and research opportunities in AI infrastructure, distributed systems, and high-performance networking, especially work that combines implementation with experimental evaluation. Outside engineering, I enjoy traveling and documenting the places I visit through my personal travel map.

Research interests: Distributed AI systems, collective communication, high-performance networking, network measurement, wireless communication, and signal processing.
