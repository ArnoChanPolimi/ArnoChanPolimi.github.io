I am a master's student in Telecommunication Engineering at Politecnico di Milano. My research focuses on communication for distributed AI training, particularly when GPUs are located in different datacenters. I study how communication between these GPUs can adapt as network conditions change.

I received my B.Sc. in Electronic Information Engineering from the School of Information and Electronics at Beijing Institute of Technology in 2023. I was fortunate to work with Qin Zhang and his team on my undergraduate thesis, which explored cooperative transmission in wireless ad hoc networks. I used simulations to study how nodes synchronize their clocks and adjust their signals to form a directional beam.

In autumn 2025, I spent a semester at ENSEA in France on an Erasmus exchange, taking courses in computer networks, wireless communications, and network and software security.

I have also worked on carrier-grade gateway software, developing and testing C/IPsec functionality. My academic projects include recommender systems, video- and radar-based vital-sign sensing, and wireless communication. I enjoy the practical side of this work: implementing an idea, finding out where it breaks, and using measurements to understand why.

I am looking for internship and research opportunities in AI infrastructure, distributed systems, and high-performance networking. Outside my studies, I enjoy traveling and keep a map of the places I have visited.

Research

Master's Thesis: Collective Communication and WAN Adaptation
I use PyTorch FSDP, NCCL/MSCCL, and RoCEv2 RDMA to study communication across datacenters. My thesis investigates how All-Gather schedules and network paths can be adjusted together as available bandwidth changes, using network measurements to guide and evaluate those decisions.
